2Frequently asked questions

How do I create an account?

You can sign up with email and password, Google, or Sign in with Apple. You'll be asked for your first and last name, governorate and gender, and you must confirm you're at least 16 years old and accept our Terms and Privacy Policy.

I can't sign in or forgot my password

Check that you're using the same method you signed up with (email, Google or Apple). If you've forgotten your password, use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. If you still can't get in, email us from the address linked to your account and we'll help.

Can I use Piwi without an account?

Yes. Guest Mode lets you browse races and leaderboards without creating an account. To follow races or athletes, add results or build a profile, you'll need to sign up.

How do I edit my profile?

Open the Profile tab and go to Settings to update your details, photo and optional social links (Instagram, Facebook, Strava).

How do I delete my account?
  1. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm the deletion.

Your account and personal data are deleted immediately, including your result claims, evidence photos, wishlist, follows, blocks and notifications. If you signed in with Apple, the connection is revoked. Results already published on public leaderboards may be kept in anonymized form that no longer identifies you. You can also request deletion by emailing us.

How do I add a race result?
  1. Tap Add Result on your profile.
  2. Search for the race and choose the edition and distance.
  3. Enter your finish time, optional bib number, and upload 1–3 proof photos.
  4. Submit. An admin will review your claim.

Once approved, you'll get a notification and the result is added to your race history. It's also checked against your personal record for that sport and distance.

How long does result approval take, and why was mine rejected?

Results are reviewed manually by our team. We may ask for more evidence before approving or rejecting a claim. Claims can be rejected if the proof photo doesn't clearly match the race, distance or time, or if the details look inaccurate. Email us if you think a decision was a mistake.

Can I edit or delete a result after submitting it?

Not currently. Results can't be edited or deleted in the app after submission. If you made a mistake, email us with the race, distance and the correction, and we'll help.

My PR or race count looks wrong

PRs are calculated per sport and distance from your approved results only, so a pending claim won't appear yet. If an approved result still looks wrong, email us with the race name and distance.

How do I share my result?

After a result is approved, Piwi can generate a branded result certificate with your name, race, time and ranks that you can share to social platforms.

How do leaderboards work?

There is a national leaderboard for each sport × distance combination (for example Road 10K or Trail 8K), based on your personal record. You can filter by gender and governorate.

Can I hide my name from the leaderboard?

Yes. Turn on Hide on Leaderboard in Settings. Your name and avatar are hidden and your result appears under a private-user label. Your rank and finish time remain visible.

A race's information is missing or wrong

Race details are added and maintained by the Piwi team and may not reflect last-minute changes. Always confirm dates and registration details with the race organizer. You can email us a correction, or suggest a new race with the details and a source link, and we'll update it.

Can I register for races inside Piwi?

Not yet. Registration still happens with the race organizer. Piwi links out to their registration pages where available.

What does following a race do?

Following a race adds it to My Races and notifies you about supported updates, such as when the race-day schedule or bib collection information is published.

How do I manage notifications?

Piwi sends notifications about approved results, new PRs, badges, followers and followed-race updates. You can turn push notifications on or off in the app's Settings, and in your device's system settings. Turning them off clears your push token from our servers.

How do I report or block another user?

Open the user's profile and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides their content from you and yours from them, and you can manage blocked users in Settings. The person you report is not told who reported them.

What data does Piwi collect and can I turn off analytics?

We collect the information needed to run your account, results and leaderboards. We don't collect GPS location, contacts or payment information, and we don't track you across other apps or use your data for advertising. You can turn off anonymous analytics in Settings → Analytics. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I request a copy of my data or other privacy rights?

Email piwi.support@gmail.com from your account email to request access, portability, restriction, objection or withdrawal of consent. Deletion is available directly in the app.

What is the minimum age to use Piwi?

You must be at least 16 years old. If we learn that a user is under 16, we close the account and delete the associated data.
